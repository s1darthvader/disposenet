agent: good afternoon, my name is priya from cashflow loans. am i speaking with mrs. neha sharma regarding your personal loan account number pl-1234567? customer: yes, this is neha speaking. what is it about? agent: ma'am, your emi of rupees five thousand five hundred for your loan account pl-1234567, which was due on october 5th, is still showing as outstanding in our records. customer: oh, five thousand five hundred? but i already paid my emi! it was rupees five thousand, and i made the payment on october 4th itself, well before the due date. why is there an extra five hundred rupees showing? i don't recall any extra charges. agent: ma'am, according to our system, there is an amount of five hundred rupees applied as a late payment charge and interest since the payment reflected late in our system, showing it was not cleared by the due date. the total outstanding is currently five thousand five hundred. customer: no, this is wrong. i definitely paid on the 4th, i even have the sms confirmation. why should i pay a late fee when i paid on time? can you please check your records properly? i would also like to see a full statement or a ledger entry for this payment and the charges, i want to understand how this extra amount came about. agent: understood, ma'am. i can see you are disputing this charge. i will certainly send you an updated statement showing the transaction details and the applied charges to your registered email address. please allow 24 hours for it to reach you. until then, your current outstanding will remain under dispute. customer: okay, please send it soon. i will only proceed with payment after i review the statement and understand these charges. if i paid on time, then this extra five hundred should not be there. agent: noted, ma'am. we will send the statement and wait for your review. we will call you again after 24-48 hours to follow up. thank you for your time. customer: thank you.
