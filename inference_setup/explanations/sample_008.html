L2 Disposition: Legal / Escalation – Escalation Request
Positive (supports class)   Negative (suppresses class)
agent: namaste, am i speaking with rajesh kumar? customer: haan, yes, speaking. who is this? agent: sir, this is priya calling from lakshmi finance. i'm calling regarding your loan account ending in 4287. you have an outstanding balance of rs 1,47,500 and your emi of rs 12,000 was due on the 15th of last month. i wanted to understand your situation and see how we can help you clear this. customer: see, i've already told someone from your office about this. i've been called four times this week alone. four times! it's harassment, yaar. i'm busy with my work, and every time my phone rings, it's you people. agent: i understand, sir, and i apologise for the inconvenience. but we do need to resolve this outstanding amount. can you tell me when you'll be able to make the payment? customer: that's the thing — i don't have the money right now. my salary was supposed to come on the 10th, but there's some delay with the company. the finance department said maybe by next week, but i can't promise anything. and honestly, i'm tired of explaining this to different people every single time. agent: i completely understand, sir. so once your salary comes, you'll be able to clear the dues? customer: maybe. but look, i want to speak to someone senior about this. a manager or someone from the grievance team. i don't want to keep repeating myself to every agent who calls. this is becoming very frustrating. i want written communication only — email or something official. can you arrange that? agent: of course, sir. i can note that down for you. but before i escalate, can i just confirm your current email address so we can send you official correspondence? customer: it's rajesh.kumar1985 at gmail dot com. but i'm serious — i want this handled by someone at a higher level. not just another junior agent calling me every other day. and i want everything in writing so there's a proper record. agent: absolutely, sir. i've made a note of your request. i'm going to escalate this to our senior collections team and our grievance cell right away. they will be in touch with you within 24 hours, and all future communication will be via email unless you specifically request a call. we will also ensure that your account is flagged to reduce the call frequency while your case is being reviewed at the senior level. customer: thank you. and please make sure it's actually done this time. i don't want to receive three more calls before anyone senior gets back to me. agent: you have my word, sir. i'm creating an escalation ticket right now, and it will be flagged as urgent. the grievance cell will contact you by email within 24 hours, and they'll take it from there. is there anything else i should note before i close this ticket? customer: no, that's all. just please make sure someone actually calls me back. and remember — email only going forward. agent: noted, sir. thank you for your patience, and i sincerely apologize for the repeated calls. your escalation is now live in the system. you'll hear from our senior team very soon. have a good day.
